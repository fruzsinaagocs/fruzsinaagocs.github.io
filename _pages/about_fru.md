I'm an Assistant Professor in Computer Science at CU Boulder (since August
2024) and the PI of this group.

My reseach focuses on the development of adaptive, high-order accurate
algorithms for solving challenging ordinary and partial differential equations
(ODEs and PDEs) that occur in physical systems, such as
acoustic/electromagnetic scattering, and the evolution of the early-universe.

Prior to working at CU Boulder, I was a research fellow at the Center for
Computational Mathematics in the Flatiron Institute, working with Alex Barnett.
Before that, I earned my PhD in Cosmology from the University of Cambridge
(UK), where I was advised by Anthony Lasenby, Mike Hobson, and Will Handley. I
received an MSci + BA from the same university.
