**Andy Gusty** earned his BS in applied mathematics and computer science from
CU Boulder in 2026. In his last year at CU, he completed his senior thesis in
the group, co-advised by Emily Jensen, Cody Scarborough, and Elizabeth Bradley.
His thesis, titled "Optimal Design of Nonlinear Periodic Electrical Networks
for Soliton Pulse Generation", presents a computational framework for designing
nonlinear transmission lines that support localized, large-amplitude solitons
(traveling wave solutions over an infinite, periodic circuit). 

For his senior thesis, Andy received the Undergraduate Research Award from the
College of Engineering and Applied Science. He was also awarded the prestigious
NSF Graduate Research Fellowship. 

He is now a PhD student at UC San Diego.
