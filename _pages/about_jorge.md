**Jorge Lopez Leon** is an undergraduate student studying physics at the University of Puerto Rico at Mayaguez. He completed a summer research internship in the group in 2026, as part of the Puerto Rico - Colorado Program (PRCP), where he worked on a novel numerical method for solving nonlinear, highly oscillatory ordinary differential equations. He successfully implemented an interative method based on the Poincare-Lindstedt approximation for finding periodic solutions of such equations. 

For his summer work, he was awarded the Best Poster prize in the category "Computing, Robotics, and Engineering Systems". 

He is proficient in C/C++, MATLAB, Python, and has completed several internships, including one at Sandia National Labs.
