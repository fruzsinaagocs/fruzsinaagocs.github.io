Getting involved in the CompTools group

---

If you are interested in a research position (at any level) in the CompTools group, please
first familiarize yourself with our recent research (e.g. through google
scholar) and the goals of the group (which you can find on my website).

If you are a current CU Master's or PhD student, taking the graduate class
[CSCI 5526: Computational Tools for Multiscale Problems](https://cu-comptools.github.io/fall2026)
is a great way to get a hands-on introduction to my research field.


If you are applying to be a PhD student in the lab, you must apply to the CS
PhD program at CU Boulder (instructions and resources for applying can be found
at the department page) and list me as a potential advisor in the application
form. In your application, please make it clear how your research interests
align with the broader goals of the group, and describe how your previous
experiences will help you achieve your research goals for your PhD. To guide
you in this process, please see the following links for resources:

* [CU Boulder's CS graduate admissions homepage](https://www.colorado.edu/cs/admissions/graduate-admissions)
* CU Boulder CS program's [tips for creating an impactful application and opportunities for an application fee waiver.](https://www.colorado.edu/cs/admissions/graduate-admissions/how-apply)

If you are interested in getting involved in a different way from any of the
above, please email me* with a brief description of yourself and your interest
in the group.

*Please note that I am not able to respond to all email inquiries. Following
the guidelines on this page is the best way to increase your chance of a
response.
